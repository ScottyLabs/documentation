## Join Our Communication Platforms!

By joining ScottyLabs on [TartanConnect](https://tartanconnect.cmu.edu/scottylabs/club_signup),
you will receive an email including the invite links for Slack and Discord.

## Events & Work Sessions

We hold in-person events and work sessions during the CMU school year.
Check our [calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=admin@scottylabs.org)
for the exact location and time details.
