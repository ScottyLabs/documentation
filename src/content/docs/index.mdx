## About ScottyLabs

ScottyLabs is a student organization at Carnegie Mellon University that creates technology to improve the campus experience. We build and maintain:

- Course selection and scheduling tools
- Event management platforms
- Campus resource aggregators
- Internal infrastructure and tooling
